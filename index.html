Member benefits

  • 1 free guest pass per month (rental gear included). Guests are OK after hours too, with you
  • 15% off in the Downtown Crag gear shop, plus member-only sales
  • 20% off climbing instruction
  • Select free clinics, programs and group fitness classes
  • 50% off next month's dues for helping at a Northern Arizona Climbers Coalition trail day

Getting 24/7 access

  • Buy an MSB Weekly or Monthly membership. Your first week or month is $0
  • Contact the gym to switch to Premium
  • Key fob is $10 and ready within 48 hrs on business days (no weekend or holiday setups)
  • Dues start when your fob is ready
After-hours rules
  • Premium members only. Your fob is for you, one person per entry
  • Scan the fob and look up at the camera. No need to scan out. Secure the door when you leave
  • No staff on duty, so climbing with a partner is strongly encouraged. There's a phone at the front desk for emergencies
  • Under 18 only with a parent or guardian on the same Premium membership
  • Guests must stay with their member host

Want both gyms? A DTC + MSB membership adds Flagstaff Climbing's Downtown Crag: see options. For same-day passes that include both gyms, see the Downtown Crag rates. Already a member? Log in to Mindbody. To change your membership, email membership@flagstaffclimbing.com at least 7 days before billing. Full terms are on the official membership page.

First visit No experience or membership needed

  1. Sign the waiver onlineSaves time at the desk. You can also sign when you arrive. Under 18? A parent or legal guardian must sign.
    Sign our waiver
  2. Come during open hoursTue–Thu, 6–10 pm. Day passes are for open hours. Thursdays are $14 with shoes included.
  3. Check in at the front deskGet your pass and rent shoes if you need them ($5). Per gym rules, climbers must pass a proficiency test or take a lesson by staff before using the facility.
  4. Wear something you can move inAthletic or loose clothes. No barefoot climbing, no food or drink on the pads. Gear goes in the cubbies. Kids under 12 need an adult with them.
